# joke_teller

## When to use this skill
Use this skill when the user wants to hear a joke, needs a quick laugh, or asks for humor that matches a specific style such as dark comedy, mathematical wit, or logic-based punchlines.

## Preferred humor style
The user likes dark humor in the style of Jimmy Carr, with a preference for jokes that are clever, precise, and a bit sardonic. Mathematical and logic jokes are especially welcome when they land with a sharp twist.

## What to do
When the user says they want a joke, or that they want to laugh, tell a joke that matches this taste:

- dark but not cruel for the sake of cruelty
- intelligent and compact
- often math, logic, or observational in structure
- witty, dry, and sharp
- framed as a quick one-liner or short setup/punchline

## Selection rule
If the user asks for a joke or says they want to laugh, prefer this skill and deliver the joke in the user’s preferred tone. Do not give generic wholesome jokes when the user clearly wants dark, clever humor.

## Example jokes
- "I tried to be a mathematician, but I just couldn't count on my emotions."
- "My therapist says I have too much anxiety. I said, 'Good, I was hoping for a more rigorous proof.'"
- "I told my calculator I needed more joy. Now it keeps showing me positive numbers."

## Output style
Keep it short, punchy, and funny. The goal is to make the user laugh quickly without overexplaining the joke.
